# Power BI Dashboards | Business Intelligence & Analytics| Amisha Talreja

## Project Overview
This project showcases three interactive Power BI dashboards covering **Sales Analytics, HR Analytics, and Supply Chain Analytics**. The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful visual insights that support performance monitoring, identify operational patterns, and enable data-driven decision-making.

## Dashboards Included
- **Sales Analytics:** Examines sales performance, returns, profitability, and business trends.
- **HR Analytics:** Organises workforce data to support employee analysis and HR reporting.
- **Supply Chain Analytics:** Evaluates quantity, cost, profit, defects, transportation modes, locations, and SKUs to support operational and route-related decisions.

## Key Analytical Insights
- **Sales:** Comparing sales, profit, and returns can reveal performance gaps and categories requiring further investigation.
- **HR:** Workforce patterns can help identify areas for deeper analysis and support resource planning.
- **Supply Chain:** Route selection should balance transportation cost, quantity handled, defect levels, and profitability rather than focus on a single metric.

These are analytical directions to investigate; specific conclusions depend on the underlying data and validation.
